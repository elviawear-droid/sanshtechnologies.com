Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal information Sansh Technologies collects through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

Sansh Technologies is a software design and engineering company with offices in Indore, India and Texas, US. We are responsible for the information you send us through this website.

For anything about your information, email contact@sanshtechnologies.com.

What we collect

When you use the contact form we receive your name, your email address, your company if you give it, the topics you tick and your message. We collect nothing else through the form.

When you apply for a job we receive your name, email address, phone number, years of experience, your CV and the voice introduction you record or upload, and which role you applied for.

This website uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. It is never sent to us.

Why we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry and, if we go on to work together, to prepare and deliver that work. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not sell or share your details for marketing.

We use a job application only to consider you for the role you applied for, and to contact you about it.

We rely on your consent, given when you send the form, and on our legitimate interest in answering the people who contact us.

Who else handles it

EmailJS sends form messages to our inbox, and Supabase keeps a copy in our private database so we can manage enquiries. Job applications, CVs and voice introductions are stored privately in Supabase and are seen only by the people at Sansh Technologies who handle hiring. Our email is hosted by Zoho. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which keeps technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure the service.

These providers may process information in the United States and other countries. They handle it only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep an enquiry for as long as we need it to reply and follow up. If it does not lead to work together, we delete it within 24 months. If it does, we keep it for as long as the work and our legal obligations require.

If a job application is not successful, we delete it, with the CV and voice introduction, within 12 months. If we hire you, it becomes part of your employment record.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email contact@sanshtechnologies.com and we will reply within 30 days.

You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
